{{APP_NAME}} does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no crash-report uploads. Everything the app processes stays on your device.

2. Local storage

{{APP_NAME}} contains no analytics, no usage counters, no remote configuration, and no background network requests. It does not require an internet connection to work, and it does not send diagnostics anywhere.
